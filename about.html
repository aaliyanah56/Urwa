The story behind the sisterhood

One faith. One sisterhood. One journey towards Allah.

URWA Sisterhood was born from a vision of bringing Muslim women together through the love of Allah and His Qur’an. A space where sisters learn, grow, support one another and build friendships rooted in faith. More than a community, URWA is an amanah — a shared journey of knowledge, sisterhood and service, creating a lasting legacy for generations to come.

Our beginning

A small circle with a shared intention.

URWA began with five sisters who wanted the Qur’an to remain part of everyday life, not only Ramadan. In Shawwal 1442, it grew into twelve sisters gathering for their first halaqah by the permission of Allah, that beginning grew into a wider community connected by the Book of Allah.

Our journey

One step at a time.

From the first gathering to a wider learning community, each stage has been an opportunity to learn, serve and grow.

2021

The first halaqah

Twelve sisters gathered for the first URWA halaqah.

2022

A growing circle

The community grew to approximately fifty members.

2023–2024

Learning and gathering

Activities included the Day of Arafah, Eid celebrations, Memorise and Rise, and December retreats.

2025

More structure

Structured learning levels, teams and brothers’ halaqahs became part of the journey.

2026

A wider community

The URWA community continued growing, reaching more than six hundred members.

Our direction

Our vision, mission and purpose.

Our vision

Creating meaningful connections with the Qur’an and its people, fostering growth and empowering a community that lives by its teachings.

Our mission

To have the Qur’an recited in every Muslim home in Kenya, touching homes around the globe and transforming them into stars on earth.

Our purpose

To bring people together through Qur’an learning, beneficial knowledge and a shared commitment to living by its guidance.

What we stand for

Principles that shape our community.

Qur’an-centred lives

Keeping recitation, reflection and connection with the Qur’an at the heart of everyday life.

Ikhlas

Striving to make our efforts sincere and for the sake of Allah.

Community and unity

Encouraging one another and growing through beneficial companionship.

Inclusivity

Welcoming learners at different stages, backgrounds and locations.

Personal growth

Making space for continuous learning, reflection and improvement.
