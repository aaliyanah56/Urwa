A learning rhythm that fits real life.

Find out about URWA class pathways and session arrangements. Current schedules and available spaces are shared by the administration.

Class pathways

Our learning is organised around different goals and levels. Ask the team about placement before joining a class.

Qaida

For beginners learning Arabic letters, pronunciation and the foundations of Qur’an reading.

Level 1 – Foundation

Building strong foundations in Qur’an reading, fluency and basic Tajweed application.

Level 2 – Growth

Developing reading confidence, improving Tajweed and strengthening Qur’an recitation skills.

Level 3 – Mastery

Advancing Qur’an recitation, refining Tajweed and working towards excellence and consistency.

How class placement works

  1. Send an enrolment enquiry.
  2. Share your learning goals and previous experience.
  3. The team advises on the suitable programme or level.
  4. Receive confirmed class details and next steps.

Need help choosing?

Tell us whether your goal is recitation, Tajweed, memorisation or understanding the Qur’an. We’ll guide you towards the appropriate enquiry route.
